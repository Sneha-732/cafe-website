THE HEART OF VELVET BLOOM

It started with
a little dream.

A place where coffee is never rushed, strangers become regulars, and the ordinary feels special.

A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDERS

We wanted to create
a place that felt
like exhaling.

Velvet Bloom began with a simple thought: life is better when we make a little space for it. Space for a proper breakfast, an unhurried conversation, a quiet afternoon with a book, or the first sip of something wonderful.

So we brought together the things we love most — honest ingredients, carefully made coffee, seasonal baking, and the kind of hospitality that makes you feel like you belong.

Today, our café is still guided by that same idea. We’re here for the everyday rituals and the unexpected moments, for old friends and first visits, for the days that need a little lift.

With warmth, The Velvet Bloom team
